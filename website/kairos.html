01 · Origins

Leipzig born. Berlin built.

Born Leipzig, 1993. Moved to Berlin 2018 with a sampler and forty hours of recorded subway sound. The first track came out six months later. He hasn't slept much since.

There was a particular point in 2019 — Berghain, his first set, six in the morning, ninety minutes into a four-hour block. He remembers nothing of it. But the recording came back at 138 BPM and it sounded like a city breathing.

He doesn't write tracks. He builds containers. Each release is an attempt to make a room where time behaves differently — slower, denser, more honest about its own weight. Some work. Some don't. That's the work.

I'm not making music. I'm building containers for time.
— KAIROS, NULLZONE interview, 2024

03 · Process

Four walls. Felt panels.

The studio is in Kreuzberg, four walls treated with felt panels salvaged from an old theater. A Moog Subharmonicon. A Korg Wavestate. A modular cabinet with four rows. No DAW until the final week — everything starts in hardware.

He writes at night, mixes at dawn, sleeps when he can. The process is closed to nobody. Most days he posts a snippet to his Subscribers thirty seconds long, raw, unfinished.
