Studio AURELYS · 2026
Transparency

Three promises. Written, not whispered.

01 / Privacy
Local-first by default.

Your voice, your memory, your patterns — encrypted on your device. Cloud sync is opt-in, end-to-end. We can't read your data even if you wanted us to.

02 / Training
Never used to train models.

Not by us, not by any partner. Inference happens on-device where possible. When it doesn't, the request is ephemeral — discarded the moment it returns.

03 / Exit
Yours to leave with.

Export your full memory in plain text, anytime. If AURELYS disappears tomorrow, your years of reflection don't disappear with it.
